the ledger
~/…/cfo/ledger.db · SQLite

Every figure the agent says comes from a field here. Not from its own addition, not from a number earlier in the conversation. A model that adds a column will one day add it wrong, in the same confident voice.

Spending and the account are different questions. A card purchase is spending today and leaves the account when the invoice is paid. Paying it is a transfer, not an expense.

The day is yours, not UTC's. Every row stores the instant and your local day, so an expense at 22:00 is not tomorrow's — and on the 31st, not next month's.

It is a file on your machine. No server, no account, nothing synced.
